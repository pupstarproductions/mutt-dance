M.U.T.T. — Move Ur Tails Together

Wristband Policy

Pick up a wristband at the door to signal your comfort level. Each event uses a unique green and red pattern — see door signage for the specific design at this event.

The Bands

Green · Unique Pattern
I'm Friendly — Interactions Welcome
Feel free to say hi, dance, or start a conversation. Always ask before touching.
Yellow · Solid
I'm Shy — Ask If I'd Like to Interact
I may want to engage but need a gentle check-in first. Please ask before approaching.
Red · Unique Pattern
I Will Initiate When I'm Ready
Do not approach me for interaction. I know you're here — I'll come to you when I'm ready.
Blue · Solid
Event Staff
Find us if you need help. We are here for you. Zero tolerance enforced.

No Wristband?

At tonight's event: if someone has no wristband, they are assumed to be bar staff. If they are not bar staff and don't have a wristband, please alert M.U.T.T. staff (look for a solid blue band).

Consent can be withdrawn at any time, regardless of wristband color. If your consent is violated, notify event staff immediately.
