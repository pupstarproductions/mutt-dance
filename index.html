About

M.U.T.T. is a furry dance party series based in Connecticut, built on the PLURR philosophy: Peace, Love, Unity, Respect, and Responsibility. We're furry-rooted and everyone-welcome — bring your ears, your tail, your kandi, yourself.

Events are 21+. Music is the headliner. The vibe is yours to make.
