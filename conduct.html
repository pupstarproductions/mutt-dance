The Rules

🚫
No Sexual Activity
M.U.T.T. is a fetish-friendly dance party, not a play party. Sexual activity is not permitted anywhere in the space. Keep it on the dance floor.
👗
Dress Code
Leather, latex, fursuits, pup gear, and ABDL wear are highly encouraged as long as genitals remain covered. Express yourself — just keep it venue-legal.
🤝
Respect Everyone
This means attendees, performers, bartenders, and event staff alike. We are all here to have a good time. Treat every person in this space with dignity.
🛡️
Safety Is Our #1 Priority
Check in with each other. Do not put yourself or others in danger. If you see something, say something — talk to event staff if you have a concern about anyone's wellbeing.
⚠️
Zero Tolerance Policy
Breaking these rules — or a history of consent violations coming to light — may result in removal from this event and future events. Talk to event staff if you have a problem with an individual.

Thank you, and have fun! 🐾
