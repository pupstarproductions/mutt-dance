M.U.T.T. — Move Ur Tails Together

Photo Policy

Consent doesn't stop at touch — it applies to cameras too. Ask first, every time, regardless of wristband color.

The Rules

📸
Always Ask First
Ask for explicit consent before photographing or filming anyone, regardless of wristband color. A nod or smile is not consent — get a clear verbal yes.
🙅
No Means No — Immediately
If someone declines, do not photograph them. Do not try again later. Do not capture them in the background intentionally. No exceptions.
📵
Posting & Tagging
Check in before tagging anyone publicly. Some attendees are not out in all spaces. Be a good community member — when in doubt, don't post their face.
🐾
Fursuits & Costumes
Due to limited sight and mobility inside a fursuit, the wearer may not be aware of a camera or able to respond clearly. Always ask before photographing anyone in a fursuit — and accept silence or no movement as a no.
⚠️
Zero Tolerance Policy
Violations may result in immediate removal from this event and future events. If someone is photographing you without consent, notify event staff immediately. Please drink responsibly. 21+ event.

If your consent is violated in any way, please tell a staff member — look for a solid blue wristband — or reach out afterward.
